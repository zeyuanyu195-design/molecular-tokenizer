Local modifications to graphbpe.py:
1. Use a package-relative utils import to avoid global sys.path/module collisions.
2. Clamp the default multiprocessing worker count to at least one.
3. Avoid division by zero in the ring-frequency report for ring-free training data.
4. Treat empty .edge and .ring files as empty vocabularies during load.
5. Add optional return_edge_existence to encode (default behavior unchanged).
   The adapter uses this matrix to reject unseen bonds instead of dropping them.
6. Build the atom-to-subgraph index in linear rather than quadratic time.
7. Stream training results and maintain a motif-to-molecules inverted index.
8. Cache motif ID lookup, join workers after training, and quiet RDKit workers.
9. Optional partition_processor hook bypasses NPE merging for BRICS+DemoDiff
   while reusing upstream node/attachment encoding and decoding.
The NPE learning/merging policy is otherwise retained. Upstream atom-property
and stereochemistry fidelity limitations remain in the DemoDiff codec; the
facade rejects mismatches in strict mode. NPE+SAFE uses only atom partitions
and original molecule properties, not the DemoDiff decoder.
