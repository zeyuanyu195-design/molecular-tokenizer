What is counted matters.

Two different units. Motif/node counts exclude connectivity overhead. SAFE text tokens include dots, connection labels and bond symbols. Their ratio is not a complete-encoding compression ratio. Both text rows use the same text tokenizer, without special tokens, padding, truncation or dropout.
Sample selection and the 5,663 changed-boundary records

SAFE changes the proposed GraphBPE boundaries in 5,663 of the 49,992 successful records. These cases are excluded from the fixed-boundary comparison. The current implementation restricts certain cuts to preserve stereochemistry; omitted cuts can involve double, single, aromatic and triple bonds.

Of the 44,329 boundary-preserving records, CHEMBL1213498 lacks a successful BRICS result, leaving 44,328 shared molecules. Across all 44,329, DemoDiff and fixed GraphBPE counts also agree record by record: 372,957 tokens, mean 8.413386.

For CHEMBL55653, skipping a double-bond cut changes four GraphBPE motifs into three SAFE fragments while preserving molecular reconstruction. Boundary change is distinct from decoding failure.

The full original-SMILES DemoDiff run encodes 49,998 of 50,000 records, with two SMILES parsing errors and zero successfully encoded rows reporting unknown nodes.

04 / SCOPE & EVIDENCE
What these results establish.
Implemented and checked
GraphBPE segmentation, SAFE serialization, molecular reconstruction and fixed-boundary motif lookup. The three examples have verified round trips.
SAFE dots separate fragments. Paired labels, their attachment positions and bond symbols specify cross-fragment connectivity.
Remaining work
A reversible model-token sequence decoded from motif IDs alone is not implemented. Reconstruction here retains the complete SAFE string. The full DemoDiff batch has not undergone a complete molecular round-trip check.
No additional frequency-based fragment vocabulary is trained in these results.
